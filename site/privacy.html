Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy covers the B-Anime app for Windows, Mac and Android.

Short version: B-Anime has no servers, accounts of its own, ads, analytics or tracking. Your library and settings stay on your device. It talks only to the services you choose to use: AniList for your lists, your own Google Drive to keep your place across devices, and the sources you add.

What stays on your device

B-Anime keeps its data in its own folder on your device (on Windows, %APPDATA%\B-Anime):

  • your library: the folders you add, the files it finds and the shows they're matched to;
  • a copy of your AniList lists, and where you stopped in each episode;
  • your settings, installed extensions and a log file for troubleshooting.

Sign-ins to AniList and Google are kept in your system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager), or in a file only your user account can read (Mac and Android). B-Anime never moves, renames or deletes your video files.

AniList

When you sign in to AniList, B-Anime reads your lists and updates your progress there, for example when you finish an episode. That's between you and AniList, under AniList's terms; B-Anime isn't affiliated with AniList.

Google sign-in

Signing in with Google is optional. It keeps your place in each show the same on all your devices. B-Anime asks Google for only three things:

  • Your email address, to show which account you're signed in with. It stays on your device.
  • A private B-Anime folder in your Google Drive (Drive's hidden app data folder). B-Anime keeps one small file there with, for each show, the latest episode you watched, where you are in it, the episode's length and when you watched it. Nothing else.
  • Sign-in itself (OpenID), to confirm it's you.

B-Anime can't see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and the folder doesn't appear in your Drive's file list. The file goes straight between your device and your Google account; it never passes through anyone else's server.

B-Anime's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to keep your place in sync between your own devices; it isn't shared, sold, used for ads or used to train AI models.

To remove it: choose Sign out in B-Anime's Settings, which ends the sign-in with Google. To delete the saved places too, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps, find B-Anime and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove B-Anime's access at any time in your Google account's third-party connections.

Other connections

  • Updates: B-Anime checks GitHub for new versions and downloads them from there.
  • Skip intro: to find where an episode's opening and ending are, it asks AniSkip with the show's ID, the episode number and its length.
  • Extensions: sources you install connect to their own sites when you use them. They're made by others; check each one before you install it.
  • Watch together: devices in a room connect to each other directly, or through iroh's public relays when they can't, which pass the encrypted connection along without reading it. Others in the room see the name you choose and what the room is playing.

Sharing

B-Anime doesn't collect, share or sell data. There are no ads, analytics or crash reports.
